## Article 18-6 — Safety Standards for Electrical and Camping Equipment of Camper Vehicles

① The electrical equipment of camper vehicles under Article 29(3) of the Motor Vehicle Management Act shall comply with the following standards: [Amended 2020-02-28, 2021-08-27, 2025-08-14]

1. The external power inlet shall be structured to prevent the ingress of water.

2. The charger shall be equipped with an overload protection function.

3. High-voltage components of DC 60 volts or AC 30 volts or more shall bear the warning label under Attached Table 5, item 4(a).

4. Power cut-off functions such as an earth leakage circuit breaker and fuses shall be provided.

② The camping equipment of camper vehicles under Article 29(3) of the Motor Vehicle Management Act shall comply with the following standards: [Added 2020-02-28, 2025-08-14]

1. Sleeping facilities (including convertible sofas) usable by a number of sleeping occupants equal to at least one-third of the seating capacity shall be provided. In this case, when calculating the number of sleeping occupants, fractions shall be rounded up, and the sleeping facility per occupant shall be at least 1,700 millimetres in length and 500 millimetres in width, or at least 8,500 square centimetres in area.

2. The camper vehicle shall be equipped with one of the following emergency escape spaces, emergency exits or windows:

a. An emergency escape space of at least 450 millimetres in width and at least 550 millimetres in height between the compartment where the driver is seated and the camping space.

b. An emergency exit or window of at least 450 millimetres in width and at least 550 millimetres in height for emergency escape, located away from the entrance of the camping space.

c. An emergency exit or window through which an ellipsoid with a horizontal axis of 610 millimetres and a vertical axis of 432 millimetres can pass without interference within the camping space.

3. Storage compartments installed in the camping space shall be equipped with a device or structure to prevent them from opening or closing while the vehicle is in motion.
